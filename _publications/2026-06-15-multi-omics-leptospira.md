This study used integrated transcriptomic and proteomic analyses of human patient samples to investigate host responses during acute Leptospira infection. The work identified key inflammatory pathways and conserved host-response factors associated with infection, providing insights into host–pathogen interactions and potential candidates for future biomarker and therapeutic research.
