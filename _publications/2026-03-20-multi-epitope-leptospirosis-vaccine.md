This study developed a multi-epitope vaccine approach against Leptospira using comparative genomics and reverse vaccinology. The vaccine candidate incorporated B- and T-cell epitopes with a TLR4 agonist and was evaluated for immunogenicity, macrophage activation, and immune responses in mice, supporting its potential as a cross-protective vaccine strategy.
