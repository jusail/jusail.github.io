This study investigated the protective effects of the gut commensal Bifidobacterium longum against Salmonella Typhimurium and Shigella flexneri using laboratory and Caenorhabditis elegans models. The findings highlight the potential of beneficial bacteria to reduce enteric pathogen infection and provide non-antibiotic approaches for controlling bacterial disease.
