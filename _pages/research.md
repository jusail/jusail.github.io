# Research

## Understanding host–pathogen interactions

My research focuses on understanding how **bacterial pathogens interact with their hosts and shape immune responses during infection**.

During my PhD at the **National Institute of Animal Biotechnology (NIAB), Hyderabad**, I use leptospirosis as a model to investigate the interaction between *Leptospira* and the host immune system.

My work brings together **experimental infection biology, immunology and multi-omics approaches** to identify host responses associated with infection and explore their biological significance.

## Current research

### Host responses to *Leptospira* infection

My doctoral research investigates how *Leptospira* infection alters host immune responses.

I work with clinical and experimental infection models and use **transcriptomics and proteomics** to identify changes in host genes, proteins and biological pathways during infection.

These discoveries are followed by experimental validation to investigate selected host factors and understand their potential contribution to the infection response.

### From molecular patterns to biological questions

A major interest of my work is what comes **after an omics experiment**.

Large-scale datasets can reveal many genes and pathways that change during infection, but an important question is:

**Which of these changes actually matter for the biology of infection?**

I am interested in combining systems-level discovery with experimental approaches to move from **infection-associated molecular changes toward testable biological mechanisms**.

## Looking beyond leptospirosis

Although my PhD research is centered on *Leptospira*, my broader scientific interest is **bacterial host–pathogen biology**.

I am interested in understanding how different bacterial pathogens interact with host cells, how pathogens influence immune responses, and how these interactions contribute to protection, persistence or disease.

For my next stage of research, I hope to develop deeper expertise in **mechanistic host–pathogen biology**, building on my experience in bacterial infection, immunology and multi-omics.

## A question that motivates my research

**How does an encounter between a pathogen and a host cell translate into an immune response—and which molecular interactions ultimately determine the outcome of infection?**
